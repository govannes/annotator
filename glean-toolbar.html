The Psychology Behind Subject Lines That Always Get Opened

The average office worker receives Headlines121 emails per day. Your subject line has about 3 seconds to earn the open. Most copywriters obsess over the body — the real battle is won before anyone reads a single word.

The most effective subject lines work because they CTAscreate an itch the reader can only scratch by opening the email. This is called the curiosity gap — one of the oldest psychological triggers in writing.

Consider the difference between "Our new product is here" and Inspo"We almost didn't launch this." The second creates a story with a missing chapter. The reader's brain wants to resolve it.

Urgency works — but Avoidonly when it's real. Fake urgency trains your list to ignore your deadlines and tanks long-term deliverability.
